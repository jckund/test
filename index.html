    Posted American odds per book, with Kalshi as source of truth. Each book's EV is the expected return on a $1 bet at that book's odds: EV = p·(payout) − (1−p), shown as a percent. This is a worst-case EV — p is Kalshi's conservative no-side chance (1 − no-ask, the low end of the quote), so it's the return you'd still clear if the true chance sits at the pessimistic edge of the market. green means the book's line is longer than Kalshi's (positive EV), red means negative. Rows are sorted by the best EV across books. Book odds include vig.

    Kalshi vs the books
    Implied odds for the top-finishing driver of each manufacturer, derived from the live Kalshi winner market. Each driver's win probability (yes-price midpoint) is normalized within its make, so P(top of make) = driver win prob ÷ sum of the make's win probs. The Which make wins card normalizes each make's total win prob across the three makes. Book Top [Make] Driver markets and the EV (expected return on a $1 bet at the book's odds) appear alongside. EV is worst-case — it prices against Kalshi's conservative no-side chance, not the displayed midpoint. Cells shaded green are +EV: the book's line is longer than the Kalshi-implied fair line.
