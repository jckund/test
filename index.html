    Posted American odds per book, with Kalshi as source of truth. Each book's Edge is the gap in implied win probability (Kalshi − book, in points): green means the book's line is longer than Kalshi's (it pays more than Kalshi's implied chance → +EV), red means shorter. Rows are sorted by the best edge across books. Book odds include vig.

    Kalshi vs the books
    Implied odds for the top-finishing driver of each manufacturer, derived from the live Kalshi winner market. Each driver's win probability (yes-price midpoint) is normalized within its make, so P(top of make) = driver win prob ÷ sum of the make's win probs. The Which make wins card normalizes each make's total win prob across the three makes. When FanDuel posts a Top [Make] Driver market, its odds and the edge (Kalshi implied − book implied, in points) appear alongside. Cells shaded green are +EV: the book's line is longer than the Kalshi-implied fair line.
